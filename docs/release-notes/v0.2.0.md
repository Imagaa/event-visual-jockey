Second pre-release of **EVJ — Event Visual Jockey**: chains, a Chroma Key with an eyedropper, and a much smoother operator window.

![EVJ in the Live layout](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Imagaa/event-visual-jockey/main/docs/screenshots/live.png)

### Download
**`EVJ-Setup-0.2.0.exe`** below — Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit, no admin rights needed. Installing over v0.1.0 is fine.

### New
- **Chains**
  - *Layer chains*: layers of one scene start one after another — when the one before ends or N seconds after it started — replacing it or overlaying it.
  - *Scene chains*: scenes play one after another when their longest clip ends; A–B loops wait for ⏭ Next.
  - Every chain stops at the end or loops. Shift+click slots (one scene → layer chain, several scenes → scene chain) or scene headers, then right-click.
- **Chroma Key with an eyedropper**: press *Pick* and click the colour on the Preview or Program monitor. Works for green / blue screens (shadows included, spill suppression for edges) and for plain white / black backgrounds of logos, on images and videos.
- **Layer 1 is the top layer** (drawn in front); ⏶ / ⏷ reorder layers; right-click to delete a layer or a scene.
- **Every image has a duration** (default 5 s, per image), with a countdown and timeline like a video.
- Countdowns with milliseconds; clearer dialogs (Enter confirms, Esc cancels, never a TAKE by accident).

### Faster
- The Program / Preview monitors in the operator window run at ~60 fps (they were stuck near 30 fps and waited for the GPU).
- The audience's output has GPU priority over the operator window.
- Thumbnails are cached: reopening a show no longer decodes every video again.
- No console window next to EVJ.

### Upgrading from v0.1.0
- Shows from v0.1.0 are converted when opened: the layers are reversed so the show looks the same with Layer 1 on top, and one-layer sequences become scene chains. **Once saved by v0.2.0, do not open the show in v0.1.0 again.**
